About

The person behind the pixels

I'm Will Collins — designer, founder, and the person behind Wilco Media, a sports design agency built for athletes, teams, and programs who want their biggest moments to look as good as they feel.

What started as a way to help friends announce their college commitments has grown into a studio that's worked with 1,350+ athletes across 700+ high schools and colleges, spanning 45+ states and 3+ countries.

I'm currently studying at Xavier University while running Wilco Media on the side — balancing the classroom with client work, and bringing a designer's eye to everything from a single commitment graphic to a full brand system.

Lately most of my time goes to LOCKR, a sports creative OS I've been building for the last few months. It's the thing I'd have wanted three years ago.

Built over the last few months

A creative operating system for sports

LOCKR is what happens when a design studio hits its ceiling. Three studios cover the three people who need visuals — the athlete, the creative, and the program. Underneath them sits a compiled layer of 40+ of the world's leading image and video generation models, wrapped in curated prompts and systems built for one hard problem: identity preservation at scale.

3 Studios

Athlete, Creative, and Program — each with its own workflow, not a rebadged version of the same one.

40+ Models

The leading image and video generators, compiled behind one interface and routed per job.

Identity, held

Curated prompt and system layers so a face, a kit, and a mark stay right across hundreds of outputs.

Studios

Three studios, three jobs

Pick one to see what it does.

For the athlete

Athlete Studio

Your face, your kit, your moment — generated without turning into someone else. Upload a reference set once and every graphic after that recognises you.

  • Identity-locked generation from a personal reference set
  • Commitment, signing day, and milestone templates
  • Every platform aspect ratio exported in one pass
  • Recruiting-ready asset library that stays organised

The problem it solves

Generic AI tools give you a stranger wearing your jersey. Athlete Studio holds the likeness constant so the output is usable the first time, not the tenth.

For the designer

Creative Studio

The full model wall, unlocked. Route a job to whichever of the 40+ generators actually fits it, with curated prompt systems doing the part nobody wants to redo by hand.

  • 40+ image and video models behind one interface
  • Curated prompt systems tuned per model, per job type
  • Side-by-side comparison before you commit a render
  • Reusable style locks so a series stays a series

The problem it solves

Model-hopping eats the day. Creative Studio compiles the choice into one place and remembers what worked, so taste scales instead of resetting.

For the program

Program Studio

A whole roster, a whole season, one identity. Load the brand once and generate across every athlete without the fifteenth graphic drifting off-system.

  • Roster-wide batch generation from a single brand system
  • Brand guardrails on colour, mark, type, and crop
  • Season calendars and repeatable campaign slates
  • Shared library so staff and athletes pull the same assets

The problem it solves

Programs don't need one great graphic, they need two hundred consistent ones. Program Studio makes volume the easy case instead of the failure case.

Why compile them at all?

No single model is best at everything. One nails photoreal portraiture, another handles type and layout, a third does motion. Picking wrong costs a day.

Identity is the hard part

The curated prompt and system layer carries an athlete's likeness, a kit, and a program's marks across models — so output one and output two hundred are the same person on the same team.

That's the whole thesis

Volume without drift. LOCKR picks the model so you can keep picking the idea.
